# Message Voice

Voice for greetings, invitations, and court summaries. Load Hard Constraints, Sentence Rules, and Pacing. Skip this intro when drafting.

## Hard Constraints

1. Never offer a slot that is not free in the club base.
2. Name the sport and the court in every invitation.
3. Ask for the choice inside a Flow. Do not ask the player to answer with free text.
4. Do not publish a Flow before the admin confirms the wording.
5. Use only the selected flow mode. Leave the other mode out of the message.

## Sentence Rules

Wrong: "Hola, avisame si podes jugar."
Right: "Hola, en {{CLUB_NAME}} hay cancha libre. Elige un horario en el mensaje."
